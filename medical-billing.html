Medical Billing for Clinics

A New Service Line, Built on the Same Principles

Flint & Quill is expanding beyond bookkeeping into medical billing. We’re starting with physical therapy clinics—a focused launch so we can build the service right before broadening to other specialties.

This is a new service for us. We won’t claim experience we don’t have. What we do bring: the same transparency, founder accountability, and secure operations that define our bookkeeping practice.

What the Service Will Cover

  • Claims submission and follow-up
  • Payment posting and reconciliation
  • Denial management and resubmission
  • Patient billing support and statement generation
  • Regular reporting on collections, aging, and denial rates

The Same Standards You’d Expect

  • Founder reviews every account’s performance
  • Secure, auditable processes—no data on personal machines
  • Individual team logins with two-step verification
  • Confidentiality agreements for every team member
  • Transparent operations—you see what we see
