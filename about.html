About Flint & Quill

A new bookkeeping firm built for the trades. Founder-led, transparent, no shortcuts.

The Story

Flint & Quill was founded by Mansoor in Texas with a specific purpose: give home-services contractors—plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, roofers—bookkeeping they can actually rely on.

Contractors build real things. Their bookkeeping should be just as solid. That’s the idea behind the name: Flint represents the trades—stone, sparks, the work of building. Quill represents the careful record-keeping that makes the numbers true.

This is a new firm. We don’t have a decade of history to point to or a wall of logos. What we have is a clear offer, honest pricing, secure processes, and a founder who reviews every deliverable before it reaches you.

How We Work

Flint & Quill is a Texas company. You sign with us here, you can call Mansoor here, and he’s the one who reviews your books before you see them.

Our delivery team is based in Pakistan. This is how we keep monthly bookkeeping at $550/month instead of typical US bookkeeper rates. We’re transparent about this because you deserve to know exactly how your books are handled.

Here’s how your data is protected:

  • All work happens inside your own QuickBooks Online—you own the account
  • Bank access is view-only through QuickBooks’ secure bank-feed system—nobody ever has your bank password
  • Nothing is downloaded or stored on personal computers
  • Every team member uses their own login with two-step verification (MFA)
  • Every team member signs a confidentiality agreement
  • Mansoor personally checks the work before it reaches you

Our team is certified in QuickBooks Online. We work in QuickBooks every day, and we know the platform inside and out for contractor workflows—job costing, bank feeds, reconciliation, payroll integration.

What We Stand For

◆ Honesty

Transparent pricing. No hidden fees. No vague scope. We tell you what it costs and what’s included before work starts, not after. If something is outside our scope, we say so and point you to the right person.

◆ Reliability

Month-end close by business day 10, every month. If we miss it, that month is free. We put a price on our deadline because a promise without a consequence isn’t a promise.

◆ Client Ownership

Your books live in your QuickBooks. You can check our work any day. If you ever leave, everything goes with you—complete, clean, and yours. No hostage books, ever.

◆ Founder Accountability

Every deliverable is reviewed by Mansoor before it reaches you. Your books get a second pair of eyes from the person whose name is on the firm. That’s the standard, not the exception.
