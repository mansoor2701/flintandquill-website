How We Protect Your Data

Your financial data deserves careful handling. Here is exactly how we work.

🔒

Your QuickBooks, Your Account

All work happens inside your own QuickBooks Online. You own the account, you control access, and you can check our work any day.

👁

View-Only Bank Access

Bank and card feeds connect through QuickBooks’ secure bank-feed system. We never receive or store your bank passwords.

No client data is downloaded or stored on personal computers. Work stays inside your cloud-based QuickBooks.

The founder personally reviews every client’s deliverables before they reach you. Your books get a second pair of eyes, every time.
